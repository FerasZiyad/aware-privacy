Aware Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Aware is a browser extension that measures and limits the time you spend on sites you choose. It is built so that your data never leaves your device: there is no Aware server, no account, no analytics and no advertising.

What Aware stores

Everything below is kept in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device only.

Data Why
Time spent per day on the sites you add Daily limits, the report and the popup
YouTube channels you watch, with time and video counts The channel report, productive channels and channel blocking
Your YouTube subscription list, if you import it Showing Shorts and suggestions from your subscriptions only
Your settings, modes and commitments Applying the rules you set
Your Toggl API token, if you connect Toggl Reading your Toggl time entries

Aware keeps up to two years of daily history and deletes older days automatically. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

What Aware sends, and to whom

Aware sends nothing to its developer. It only talks to two services, and only for features you turn on:

  • Toggl Track (api.track.toggl.com), if you connect it. Aware sends your API token to read your profile, projects and time entries. It never creates or changes entries. Toggl's own privacy policy applies to that data.
  • YouTube (youtube.com), using your existing signed-in session, when you import your subscriptions or ask Aware to unsubscribe from a channel. Unsubscribing sends YouTube the same request its Unsubscribe button does, signed with your YouTube session cookies, only for the channels you select. The cookies are read for that request and are never stored or sent anywhere else. When you search for a channel to block in Kids mode, Aware sends the words you type to YouTube's search, without your cookies, and shows the channels it returns.

What Aware reads on pages

To enforce your rules, Aware runs on web pages and reads the address of the page and, on YouTube, the channel and video being shown. This is used on your device to count time and hide or block content, and it is not recorded beyond the data listed above.

Exports

The CSV exports are created on your device and saved wherever you choose. Aware does not upload them.

Sale and sharing

Aware does not sell, transfer or share your data with anyone, does not use it for advertising or creditworthiness, and does not use it for anything other than the features described here.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
